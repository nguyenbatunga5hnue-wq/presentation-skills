Bài gợi ý

Sport attracted 26% of 10–15-year-olds, equalling cartoons as their favourite programme. This figure almost halved to 14% among viewers aged 16–20, and only 4% of 21–25-year-olds chose sport, the lowest figure in all three charts. News showed the opposite pattern: just 6% of the youngest group watched it, compared with 18% of 16–20-year-olds. Among those aged 21–25, news was the most watched programme, at a third (33%), more than five times the figure for the youngest viewers.
