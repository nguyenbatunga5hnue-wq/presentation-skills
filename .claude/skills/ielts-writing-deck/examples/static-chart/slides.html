Đề bài: mức nước hồ chứa ở sáu thành phố

The chart shows the water levels in reservoirs of six cities in Australia in October 2009 and October 2010.

2009: Sydney và Canberra cùng cao nhất (75%), Melbourne thấp nhất (25%)
2010: 4 / 6 thành phố tăng
Canberra: tăng mạnh nhất (+23), gần đầy, khoảng 98%
Darwin: thành phố duy nhất giảm, 45% → 38%
Brisbane: giữ nguyên 55%
2010: Melbourne và Darwin cùng thấp nhất, 38%
Overview: 4 tăng · 1 giảm · 1 giữ nguyên. Có 2 mốc thời gian nên được dùng rose, fell, remained unchanged.

Bài 1 · đoạn thân bài

Bài của em
In October 2009, Melbourne had the lowest water level, compared with 40% in Perth, and both Syden and Canberra had the highest water levels in reservoirs, at 75%. In October 2010, the figure in all cities had risen. Melbourne and Perth surged to 38% and 60%, while Sydney saw a modest rise to 80%. Canberra experienced the most significant growth, which had the highest figure compared to other 6 cities, reached at approximately 98%. […]
Đoạn sửa
In October 2009, Melbourne had the lowest reservoir level, at 25%, followed by Perth at 40%, while Sydney and Canberra jointly recorded the highest figure, at 75% each. By October 2010, four of the six cities had seen an increase. The figures for Melbourne and Perth surged to 38% and 60% respectively, while Sydney saw a modest rise to 80%. Canberra experienced the most significant growth, reaching approximately 98%, the highest figure of all six cities. By contrast, Darwin was the only city to see a decline, from 45% to 38%, the same level as Melbourne, whereas Brisbane remained unchanged at 55%.
Bấm một lỗi được gạch đỏ.

Bài 1 · điểm đoạn trích

Đoạn thân bài (trích 1 đoạn, chấm theo đoạn)

  • TA: sai “all cities had risen”, bỏ sót Darwin và Brisbane, thiếu số của Melbourne
  • GRA: sai “reached at”, “which” trỏ sai, sai thì “had risen”
  • LR: surged, modest rise dùng tốt; sai chính tả “Syden”
  • C&C: thiếu “respectively”, chưa gom các thành phố theo nhóm tăng / giảm

Bản gợi ý
Overall, reservoir levels rose in most cities over the period, with Darwin being the only city to see a decline and Brisbane remaining unchanged. Canberra had the highest level by October 2010, whereas Melbourne’s reservoirs were consistently among the least full.

Đoạn gợi ý
Four of the six cities saw their reservoir levels rise over the period. Most notably, Canberra’s figure jumped from 75% to almost full capacity, at around 98%, making it the highest of all six cities in October 2010. By contrast, Sydney, which had shared the top position with Canberra in 2009, saw only a marginal rise from 75% to 80%.

Viết cả bài: biểu đồ game điện tử

IntroductionThe pie charts illustrate the characteristics of electronic game players in South Korea in 2003, covering their age, gender and preferred game types.

OverviewOverall, players were predominantly young, and males made up the vast majority. Action games were by far the most popular genre, whereas role-playing and educational games attracted the smallest shares.

Body 1Players under 18 and those aged 18 to 36 were almost equal in number, at 39% and 38% respectively, together accounting for over three-quarters of the total. Players above 36 made up the remaining 23%. In terms of gender, males constituted 72% of players, roughly two and a half times the proportion of females, who accounted for 28%.

Body 2Action games were the favourite genre, with 43% of players, which exceeded sports games (20%) by 23 percentage points. Racing games attracted 17%, while role-playing and educational games each captured 7% and other genres 6%. Taken together, these three smaller categories matched the share of sports games alone.
