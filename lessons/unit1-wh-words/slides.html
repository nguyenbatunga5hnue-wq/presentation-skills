Technique: Wh-words

To give a longer answer, you can use Wh-words.

You should think about: What, Why, Where, When, Who, or even How.

WhatWhyWhereWhenWhoHow

Mở rộng câu trả lời bằng Wh-words

Do you like chocolate?Yes, I adore it!
WhatCái gì

My favourite is dark chocolate.

WhyVì sao

because I have a sweet tooth.

WhereỞ đâu

I buy it at a shop within walking distance of my house.

WhenKhi nào

I usually eat a little after dinner.

WhoVới ai

I often share it with my little brother.

HowThế nào

It makes me feel happy and energetic.
