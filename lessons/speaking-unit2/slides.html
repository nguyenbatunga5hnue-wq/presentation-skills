Discourse markers

Discourse markers

Discourse markers are words or phrases that connect, organise and manage what we say. In the exam, they help structure your answers and make your points clearer and more coherent.

Causes & effects

Thanks toAs a result (of)ConsequentlyThis has the effect of

Thanks to my geography teacher, I got into maps. As a result, I love travelling.

Comparing & contrasting

WhereasOn the contraryInstead ofOtherwise

My sister is a social butterfly, whereas I'm quite reserved.

Similarities

LikewiseIn the same wayIn a similar fashionJust as

Just as my grandparents did, we carry on the tradition of making bánh chưng.

Referring to a previous argument

Like I saidAs I said earlierAs I mentioned before

As I mentioned before, I've always been fascinated by space.
