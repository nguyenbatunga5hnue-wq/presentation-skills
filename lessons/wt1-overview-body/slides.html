Overview không có xu hướng: ba biểu đồ tròn

Câu 1 · Order: cao nhất ở đâuOverall, it is noticeable that while sport and cartoons were the most popular programmes among 10–15-year-olds, drama and news were favoured most by the 16–20 and 21–25 age groups respectively.

Body 1 (viết ngang): Manufacturing + Agriculture

Câu 1 · Điểm bắt đầu + so sánhIn 1960, manufacturing was the largest sector, employing 15 million people.

Câu 2 · Hai chặng xu hướng + điểm kết thúcAfter rising to a peak of 20 million in 1980, this figure fell steadily, ending at 13 million in 2020, only the third-largest.

Câu 3 · Đối tượng 2: điểm bắt đầu + so sánhAgriculture, by contrast, started at 6 million, the same as retail, before halving to 3 million by 1980.

Câu 4 · Xu hướng tiếp + điểm kết thúc + so sánhIt then remained stable for two decades and dipped to just 2 million in 2020, the lowest of all four sectors.

Body 2 (viết ngang): Retail + Healthcare

Câu 1 · Điểm bắt đầu của 2 đối tượng + so sánhRetail employed 6 million people in 1960, three times as many as healthcare, which had just 2 million jobs.

Câu 2 · Retail: hai chặng xu hướngOver the following four decades, retail jobs rose steadily to 15 million, after which the figure increased only slightly to 16 million.

Câu 3 · Healthcare: xu hướng + so sánhHealthcare saw an even more dramatic rise, more than doubling every twenty years to reach 11 million in 2000.

Câu 4 · Điểm kết thúc + vượt nhau / bằng nhauBy 2020, it had climbed to 16 million, equalling the figure for retail and overtaking manufacturing.

Body 1 (cách 2): Food and drinks + Housing

Câu 1 · Điểm bắt đầu + xếp hạngIn 1995, food and drinks accounted for the largest share of household spending, at 32%, followed by housing at 28%.

Câu 2 · Câu chuyển: báo hiệu xu hướng ngượcOver the next ten years, however, these two categories exhibited opposite trends.

Câu 3 · Đối tượng 1: xu hướng + kết thúc + vượtThe proportion spent on housing rose by 6 percentage points to 34%, making it the biggest expense in 2005.

Câu 4 · Đối tượng 2: xu hướng + kết thúc + xếp hạngIn contrast, spending on food and drinks fell to just under a quarter (24%), dropping to second place.

Body 1 (viết dọc): Sport + News

Câu 1 · Sport ở nhóm trẻ nhất + so sánhSport attracted 26% of 10–15-year-olds, equalling cartoons as their favourite programme.

Câu 2 · Sport ở 2 nhóm còn lạiThis figure was almost halved to 14% for viewers aged 16–20, and a mere 4% of the oldest group watched sport, the lowest figure in all three charts.

Câu 3 · News: báo hiệu pattern ngượcNews showed the opposite pattern: just 6% of the youngest group chose it, compared with 18% of 16–20-year-olds.

Câu 4 · News ở nhóm lớn nhất + so sánhAmong those aged 21–25, news was the most watched programme, at a third (33%), more than five times the figure for the youngest viewers.

Bài 4 · Viết Body 2

Nhóm lẻ · Bar chart
Other goods and services + Transport (25% → 23%; 15% → 19%). Viết 3 câu, có ít nhất 2 cấu trúc so sánh.
Bài mẫu AThe remaining two categories made up smaller proportions. Other goods and services accounted for a quarter of spending in 1995, considerably more than transport, at 15%. Over the next decade, the former decreased marginally to 23%, whereas the share spent on transport rose to 19%; however, it was still the smallest category.
Nhóm chẵn · Pie charts
Drama + Film + Cartoon. Viết dọc theo từng chương trình, 3 câu.
Bài mẫu BDrama was the favourite programme of 16–20-year-olds, at 30%, while it accounted for a similar proportion of the other two groups (23% and 22%). Film was consistently watched by around a fifth of each group, ranging from 19% to 23%. Cartoons were most popular among the youngest viewers (26%), dropping to 15% among 16–20-year-olds before rising to a fifth of those aged 21–25.
